## (e)
Multi-headed attention overcomes the instability of single-headed attention by allowing different heads to specialize in retrieving different parts of the input independently. In single-headed attention, keys compete for attention weights in a single softmax distribution, so noise (variance) in one key's magnitude can destabilize the entire output. Multi-headed attention decouples this competition: separate heads can attend to specific orthogonal subspaces without interference from noise in other subspaces, and averaging their outputs significantly reduces the overall variance, leading to a stable representation of the aggregate information.
