# 1.Understanding word2vec
## (a)
Given that the true distribution $y$ is a one-hot vector, $\displaystyle -\sum_{w \in Vocab} y_w \log(\hat{y}_w) = -\log(\hat{y}_o)$, where $o$ is the index of the true word. 
## (b)
+ $\displaystyle \frac{\partial J_{naive-softmax}(v_c, o, U)}{\partial v_c} = -u_o + \sum_{w \in Vocab} \hat{y}_w u_w = U(\hat{y} - y)$
+ When $\hat y = y$, the gradient is zero, indicating that the predicted distribution matches the true distribution.
+ When $\hat y$ diverges from $y$, the gradient becomes non-zero, guiding the update of $v_c$ to reduce the loss.
## (c)
For example, the word "good" and "fascinating" have similar meanings, but they have different extent.However, when being normalized, they may have similar extent, which disturb the downstream tasks.
## (d)
$$ \frac{\partial J}{\partial U} = [\frac{\partial J}{\partial u_1},\frac{\partial J}{\partial u_2},...,\frac{\partial J}{\partial u_V}] $$
## (e)
+ If $i \neq o$ $$\frac{\partial J}{\partial u_1}=\hat y_i v_c$$
+ If $i = o$ $$\frac{\partial J}{\partial u_1}=(\hat y_i - 1)v_c$$
